Frequently asked questions

What are your hours?
We’re open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week at 1174 The Queensway, Etobicoke. Walk in anytime — no appointment needed.
What product categories do you carry?
Flower across five tiers (Exotic, Premium, AAA+, AA, Budget), plus pre-rolls, edibles, vapes, concentrates and accessories. Our selection changes frequently, so call or visit to confirm what’s in stock today.
Do I need to be 19 to enter?
Yes. You must be 19 or older to enter the store or browse this site, in line with Ontario law. Please bring valid government-issued photo ID.
Can I reserve online?
Yes — tap Reserve on any available product to send a pickup request. Staff confirm by text or phone, then you pay in store with valid 19+ ID. There’s no online payment and no delivery.
Where exactly are you, and is there parking?
We’re at 1174 The Queensway, Etobicoke, ON M8Z 1R5, with easy access from the QEW and Gardiner. Free evening street parking is available nearby.
